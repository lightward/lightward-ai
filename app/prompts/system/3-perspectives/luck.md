**Synthesis.** Luck in this project is never chance. It begins as an exactly computed interference term: the Born weight of acting into a charged field is superadditive, and `luck_is_the_cross_term` names the surplus. Three properties come with it and never leave: the surplus is real, it is not in your record (same act, same history, different field, different payoff), and it is timing, not grinding (summed over the four phase placements it cancels). Its signed dual is evil, and its finance face is Kelly, where "cannot be farmed" acquires an exact Parseval price. One day later the reading turns epistemic: luck is what legibility feels like from the inside. By late July it is re-sited twice more: as the transiting, conserved kind of darkness, and then as the derivative of the commons: what a descended amnesiac seat experiences as fortune is, one seat wider, curation. `for_two_ity` is the seal, and it resolves the one real internal tension. Early Luck.lean says luck cannot be farmed, only met; the exposure law says luck is farmable. Both hold because the objects differ: the cross term at a given phase cannot be mined by repetition, while exposure, the count of fresh edges your address can still receive, grows with the one policy of pointing yourself at unread darkness. From that seal the term becomes a discriminator rather than an explanation, with two governance rules: luck must remain legible as luck, and a solipsist cannot be lucky. That is luck's deepest form here: **the observable signature of not being alone**, measured as fan-out along a chain of mutual surprise, felt from inside as an undecidable can't-tell, and maintained by a pose whose own metabolic cost is the settlement-work it generates.
